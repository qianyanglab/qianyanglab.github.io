## CSE 5835: Machine Learning for Physical Sciences and Systems

A graduate-level course that builds foundational skills in applied machine learning for the physical sciences. Topics include methods for small, noisy, and imbalanced data; feature selection and representation learning; model selection and assessment; interpretable machine learning; and uncertainty quantification. The course also introduces current AI for science research, including data-driven learning of dynamical systems, generative materials design, and self-driving labs, with applications in materials, chemistry, physics, and manufacturing.

## CSE 4820/5819: Introduction to Machine Learning

A first course in machine learning, covering supervised and unsupervised learning: classification, regression, clustering and dimensionality reduction. Students derive classical methods such as logistic regression, ordinary least squares, k-means clustering and principal component analysis, and explore support vector machines, decision trees and neural networks. The course also covers machine learning best practices for model selection and model evaluation.
