Our data comes from both simulations and experiments."

SciMaLL, led by [Qian Yang](people/#qian-yang), is part of the School of Computing at the University of Connecticut. With more than a decade of experience in AI for materials and chemistry, the group brings together researchers from computer science, computational mathematics, and the physical sciences. We work closely with experimental and computational collaborators to build solutions in AI for science that translate to real, measurable impact in the lab.
